GIVING

"Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

- 2 Corinthians 9:7

We encourage everyone to give as God has blessed you - with a cheerful heart and in response to His love. Whether big or small, every gift matters to God and advances His kingdom. Tithing is an act of worship, reflecting our love for Christ, Who gave Himself for us. We give not out of obligation, but from grateful hearts honouring God and His Church, of which we are a part.
