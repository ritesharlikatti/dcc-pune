SUNDAY WORSHIP

Sunday Worship : 10.30am to 12.30pm followed by Fellowship Lunch Open to All.

Sunday School : 11 am to 12.30pm (Ages 3 - 12).

"We proclaim Him, admonishing every man and teaching every man with all wisdom, so that we may present every man complete in Christ."

- Colossians 1:28

people

"And they devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers."

- Acts 2:42

All are welcome! Our Sunday Worship Service is the highlight of our church life. Its aim is to exalt and worship the Lord Jesus Christ as we reflect on His work of salvation on behalf of sinners, His providential care in our daily life and His Lordship over our lives.

Members and visitors alike are encouraged to prepare their hearts for worship and to give to the Lord the honour due to His name.

church

Our service includes worshipful singing, a congregational prayer time, and an expository message grounded in the clear teaching of Scripture. We conclude with a time of celebration at the Lord’s Table, followed by a fellowship lunch open to all.
